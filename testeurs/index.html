    It doesn’t work?
    • “You can’t join” or “App not available”: you’re on the wrong Google account. In Google Play, tap your photo at the top right, pick the right account, then try again.
    • No Install button? Wait a few minutes after becoming a tester, then reopen the Google Play page.
    • Did you join the group? Do step 1 first, with the same Google account.
    • iPhone or PC? Google Play testing only works on Android. Tap “Play on the web” instead.
    • How to give feedback: open the game’s page in Google Play, find “Private feedback to the developer” and write a few words.

    FAQ

    I’m not a tester yet, can I join?

    Yes! This is a closed test: only Gmail addresses on our list can sign up. Send your Gmail address to the person who shared this page; once it’s added, come back and follow the steps. Meanwhile, the “Play on the web” buttons are open to everyone.

    I have an iPhone, what do I do?

    Google Play testing only works on Android. On iPhone or PC, tap “Play on the web”. Tip: in Safari, Share → “Add to Home Screen” to use it like a real app.

    Why keep the game for 14 days?

    Google asks for at least 12 testers who keep the game installed 14 days in a row before we can publish it. No need to play for hours: open it now and then, and above all don’t uninstall it.
